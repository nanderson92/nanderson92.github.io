TECHNICAL CENTER

Process windows, measurement loops, and manufacturing judgment.

I work best where a physical system is almost promising but not yet repeatable: a droplet pattern that needs quantification, a microfluidic process that needs throughput without defects, a resistance trace that needs failure logic, or a control loop that needs real thermal behavior instead of ideal setpoints.

Core habits: define the bottleneck, choose measurable outputs, separate mechanism from symptom, and turn the next experiment into a decision rule.

Tooling: microscopy, image analysis, Python, ImageJ/Fiji, JMP, embedded controls, process simulation, and shop-floor operating discipline.
