TECHNICAL CENTER OF GRAVITY

Process-development judgment, not a skills cloud.

I’m strongest where a physical system has a messy bottleneck: wetting and transport in printed electronics, flow and stabilization in microfluidics, resistance drift in reliability screens, or thermal lag in embedded controls.

Define the output. Decide what “good” means before the experiment: useful placement, usable yield, stable resistance, or repeatable temperature history.

Instrument the loop. Turn observation into a measurement workflow using microscopy, electrical testing, image analysis, logs, or controller traces.

Make the next decision visible. Convert results into accept/reject/redesign logic instead of stopping at a pretty plot.
