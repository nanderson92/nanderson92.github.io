Micromodular Printed Electronics

When microdevices are printed from a droplet, they tend to crowd at the edge. I built the measurement workflow for figuring out which substrate conditions produce useful placement.

Wet processingImage-derived metricsProcess decision
Problem Suspended microdevices raft, pin, and accumulate as the carrier liquid spreads, dries, and imbibes. Method Top/side-view microscopy plus Python/ImageJ analysis to compare substrate/backing conditions. Output A substrate-screening decision framework for reducing edge crowding and preserving interconnect access. Tools Optical microscopyImageJ/FijiPythonSubstrate screeningJMP
