## Summary

This earlier work shows where my experimental habits started: careful protocol execution, controls, troubleshooting, and small-signal measurement discipline.

## Problem / motivation

DNA mechanics at very short lengths are difficult to probe because small length scales demand careful assay design, clean controls, and precise molecular-biology execution.
