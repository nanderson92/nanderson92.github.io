## Characterization matrix

| Technique | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| SEM | Morphology / surface structure |
| XRD | Crystallinity / phase |
| XPS | Surface composition / chemical state |
| Raman | Vibrational and structural signatures |

## What I’d do next

If I built this page out as a deeper project, I would center it on one specific film and one characterization result instead of a list of techniques. The useful habit is still the same: change a process condition, measure the material response, and decide what that response means for device use.
