## Summary

This page is not a lab project. It is a transferable systems case: high-volume food-service operations taught me to think in terms of bottlenecks, quality logs, standard work, preventive maintenance, training, and real-time corrective action.

<div class="buildout-note">Systems summary — designed to support manufacturing/process-engineering applications without overcrowding the technical project grid.</div>

## Why it belongs on the portfolio

Manufacturing environments care about throughput, uptime, quality checks, people systems, repeatability, and execution under pressure. Operations leadership is one of the clearest non-lab ways I developed those instincts.
