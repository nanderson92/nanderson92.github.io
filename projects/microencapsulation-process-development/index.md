<div class="thesis-box"><h2>Throughput only mattered inside a usable operating window.</h2><p>Increased capsule-generation throughput by 20× and improved microscopy-counted usable encapsulation yield by ~50% after flow-ratio, tooling, and crosslinking adjustments.</p></div>

<section class="artifact-panel public-safe-snapshot" aria-label="Microencapsulation snapshot">
 <h2>Public-safe experiment snapshot</h2>
 <ul class="snapshot-list"><li><strong>System:</strong> flow-focusing core-shell capsule generation for ARPE-19 cell encapsulation.</li><li><strong>Inputs varied:</strong> flow ratio, tooling geometry, crosslink timing, collection conditions, and microscopy QC criteria.</li><li><strong>CTQs:</strong> generation rate, usable capsule fraction, coalescence, shell integrity, and handling stability.</li><li><strong>Decision:</strong> speed was accepted only when formation and crosslinking stayed inside the usable window.</li></ul>
</section>

## Operating-window table

| Process change | Failure mode addressed | Metric affected | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flow-ratio tuning | Unstable capsule formation and coalescence | Usable yield | ~50% improvement |
| Tooling redesign | Low generation rate | Throughput | 20× increase |
| Crosslink timing adjustment | Fragile shells and post-formation instability | Handling stability | Improved usable capsule fraction |
| Microscopy QC | Unclassified defects | Yield interpretation | Defect classes became visible |

<p class="method-note"><strong>Yield definition:</strong> usable encapsulation yield means microscopy-counted usable capsules after excluding coalesced or visibly defective capsules. The denominator should be updated with the final lab definition before publication.</p>

<div class="changed-panel"><h2>What changed because of this</h2><p>The project moved from trying to make capsules faster to defining which flow and crosslinking conditions made faster capsule generation usable.</p></div>

<div class="next-panel"><h2>Open questions / next iteration</h2><p>The next iteration is a formal operating-window map: flow ratio, dwell/crosslink timing, and collection condition on one side, usable yield and coalescence on the other. That map would turn the improved run conditions into a repeatable screen.</p></div>
