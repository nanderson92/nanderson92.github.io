## Research Identity

My research interests sit at the intersection of chemical engineering, advanced manufacturing,
interfacial transport, and scalable electronics fabrication.

## Core Themes

### Micromodular Printed Electronics

- Deposition of microscale electronic components from liquid suspensions
- Substrate effects on particle/device distribution
- Droplet evaporation, imbibition, spreading, and contact-line dynamics
- Process-window development for scalable assembly

### Wet Processing and Interfacial Transport

- Solvent-substrate interactions
- Porous media imbibition
- Coffee-ring suppression
- Wetting, pinning, and capillary transport

### Manufacturing Translation

- Moving from lab-scale behavior to reproducible process conditions
- Defining metrics for yield, placement fidelity, and uniformity
- Connecting experimental observations to engineering design rules
