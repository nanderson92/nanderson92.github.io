<section class="background-panel background-summary">
 <h2>Why process engineering</h2>
 <p>Advanced-manufacturing process development is the focus. At Filler Lab · Georgia Tech, my work builds measurement and decision workflows for micromodular printed electronics, a manufacturing concept where semiconductor devices could be produced and assembled in a smaller, faster, less facility-intensive way than conventional fabrication. Earlier work at Mayo Clinic · Rochester, MN focused on microfluidic encapsulation throughput and thermocycler automation for biological protocols.</p>
</section>

<section class="background-panel background-access">
 <h2>Why access matters</h2>
 <p>Growing up adopted from Incheon into small-town Minnesota made me notice who systems are built for. That observation is why manufacturability, not just discovery, is the question I care about.</p>
</section>
